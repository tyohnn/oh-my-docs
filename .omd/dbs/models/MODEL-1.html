MODEL-1

Content source selection

summary
How a project chooses and sticks to one handbook content source.

Intent

Every project chooses one handbook content source and treats it as the only authority for product memory.

Rules

1. Choose before inventing handbook entries. 2. If no choice is recorded, the default is the local handbook. 3. Local means a full handbook tree people and agents can browse. 4. Notion means one Home page of catalog databases under domain / planning / development sections. Related terms: Content source, Content port. Related policy: Single content source.
