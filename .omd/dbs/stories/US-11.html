US-11

Show mobile and desktop wireframes at the top of layouts

summary
As a handbook reader, I see mobile then desktop wireframes at the top of every layout and screen-state, before narrative.
PRD
PRD-7
Features

Persona

Human or agent reading a layout or screen-state HTML catalog row.

Narrative

I open a LAY-* or STA-* document and immediately see a mobile mock, then a desktop mock, then the written role/trigger notes. Position does not vary by project.

Acceptance

  • Templates put data-omd-wireframe="mobile" then data-omd-wireframe="desktop" after the header and before main.omd-doc-body.
  • Check fails if either viewport is missing, or if any .omd-wireframe appears at or after the narrative body.
  • CSS frames mobile as a phone-width device and desktop as a wide device.
