TERM-9

Information unit

summary
One navigable chunk of product information; one IA-* catalog row.

Definition

An information unit (정보 단위) is one chunk of product information a user can navigate to or act on — a hub, a collection, an object, a compose flow, a settings group, or a nav cluster. It is not the whole product sitemap.

Examples

  • Inbox, Thread, Compose, Settings / account — each is its own IA-* row
  • Not a single row titled “Information architecture” or “전체 IA” that dumps every screen

Related terms

Pages attach to the unit they belong to. Layouts and screen-states describe how a unit is shown, not the unit itself.
