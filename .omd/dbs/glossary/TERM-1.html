TERM-1

Content source

summary
The single authoritative handbook store for one project: local handbook or Notion catalogs.

Meaning

A content source is the one place that owns handbook truth for a project. Allowed choices:

  • local — the project’s handbook documents
  • notion — catalog databases on one Notion Home page

A project never has two content sources at once. Other stores may help delivery, but they are not handbook authority.
