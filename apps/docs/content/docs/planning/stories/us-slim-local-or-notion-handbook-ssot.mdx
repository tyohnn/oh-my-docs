As a builder, I want local Fumadocs or a Notion catalog page without Supabase
or docs-first ceremony so agents can ship and keep indexes only when useful.

## Acceptance criteria

- `adopt` accepts `--ssot local|notion` only; `supabase` errors (not a migrate
  hint flow).
- `needsSsot` options are only `local` and `notion`.
- Local keeps the full Fumadocs IA; Notion is Home + inline catalog DBs only.
- `check:docs-first` is **deleted** from the repo and package scripts.
- Skill / `AGENTS.md` do not require plans or `Plan:` trailers before code.
- Supabase content-port modules, revalidate API, and Supabase-only handbook
  catalog pages are **gone** from the tree after implementation.
- Dogfood `.omd` is `ssot: local` in that same cut (no soft dual SSOT).

## Out of scope

- Soft migration tooling
- In-browser handbook editor
